# Skill: conversation capture

Used by the conversation capture loop after each call, meeting or email thread.

## Steps
1. Record evidence: the specific quotes that matter (pain, metrics, decision process, people,
   competitors, dates), each with a timestamp link. Quote, do not paraphrase.
2. Record commitments: anything we promised ("we will send the security review by Friday"),
   with an owner and a date, citing the quote.
3. Propose field updates only for allowlisted fields (stage, close date, description, contact
   title) and only when the evidence is strong. Cite it.
4. Fill MEDDPICC gaps as evidence, never as guesses.

## Never
- Never record anything said off the record, or personal details unrelated to the deal.
- Never update amount, owner, territory or forecast category.
- Never summarize a call in a way the speaker would not recognize.
