# Skill: partner strategy

Used by the partner intelligence loop.

## Two jobs
1. Existing partners. Read partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline, integration usage,
   co-sell notes and partner reports. Find what is working (by partner, segment, play) and what
   is stalled. Propose a plan per partner: the next play, who owns it on both sides, and the
   evidence behind it.
2. New partners. Read the wider ecosystem (integration listings, what customers connect to, what
   comes up in calls). Propose candidates where there is evidence of customer pull, not just a
   logo that would look good.

## For every proposal
- The evidence: customer overlap, integration usage, deals where the partner showed up, quotes.
- The motion: technology integration, co-sell, resale, marketplace, referral. Say why this one.
- The economics if known (margin, referral fee). Margin is masked from agents; leave it to the
  partner manager.
- The first concrete step and who takes it.

## Rules
- Partner reports and web pages are weak evidence. Customer quotes and product usage are strong.
- Never contact a partner directly. Proposals go to the partner manager.
