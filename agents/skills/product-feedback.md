# Skill: product feedback

Used by the product feedback loop.

- Collect feature requests, bugs and objections from call quotes and tickets as evidence.
- Group them into themes. For each theme: count of distinct accounts, total ARR of those
  accounts, open pipeline that mentioned it, and three representative quotes with links.
- Separate "blocking a deal" from "nice to have", by the buyer's own words.
- Send themes to the product team as a weekly digest. Do not promise customers anything.
- Promotion metric: the share of themes the product team marks useful.
