# Skill: territory method

Used by the territory and capacity loop. Proposals only; this loop never passes level 2.

## Principles
- Fair patches: territories with comparable potential, not comparable account counts.
- Potential comes from account scores (market model loop) plus installed-base expansion
  potential. In usage businesses, product telemetry (volume, features in use, trend) is the best
  predictor.
- Segments by company size, with written rules for edge cases, and a handoff rule for accounts
  that outgrow their segment mid-year (and how credit moves with them).
- Partner territories map onto AE territories so partner and AE incentives point the same way.

## Capacity
- Start from the target. Convert reps to ramped-rep equivalents (a new rep is partly productive
  for their ramp period).
- Test proposed quotas against historical productivity by segment and territory. If quotas come
  out above what reps have historically achieved, the plan needs more reps or a lower target,
  not a stretch.
- Plan for less than full attainment across the team.

## Output
A proposed carve (territories, rules, members), a balance report (potential per territory, spread
between highest and lowest), the capacity model, and the list of accounts that change owner with
the reason for each. Every change of owner is a human decision.
